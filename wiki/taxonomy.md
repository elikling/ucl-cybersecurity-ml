## Summary

Controlled dimension labels and page-type rules are maintained in [README](README.md#knowledge-dimensions). This first edition adds no labels beyond that registry.

## Key points

- A challenge is a defensive problem; a method describes how to analyse it; data and tools are independently versioned artefacts; source pages preserve provenance.
- Generative AI support, tool-using agents and tabular foundation models are related but distinct method families in the [landscape](syntheses/research-landscape.md#generative-data-science-strands).

## Details

### Current coverage

The first edition focuses on `network-intrusion-and-anomaly-detection`, `malware-and-malicious-code`, `adversarial-machine-learning-and-ai-security`, `anomaly-detection-and-change-point-methods`, `agents-and-tool-using-systems`, `generative-and-foundation-models` and evidence dimensions. Other taxonomy labels remain available but have not yet been researched as independent topic pages. Do not infer absence of research from absence of a local page.
