## Summary

The [2025 survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.04023) maps 45 LLM-based data-science agent systems across six lifecycle stages, from problem understanding and acquisition through analysis, modelling, interpretation and monitoring. It is cross-domain rather than a cybersecurity evaluation.

## Key points

- The authors report concentration on exploratory analysis and modelling, with less coverage of problem understanding, deployment and monitoring.
- Their abstract reports that over 90% of the reviewed systems lack explicit trust and safety mechanisms; this is a survey classification, not an independently replicated experiment.
- The survey provides a way to define a testable "meta-statistician" role, but no evidence here that an agent improves cyber defence outcomes.

## Details

### Research use and limits

Use the lifecycle as a checklist for an audited cyber analytics workflow: task framing, lawful data access, leakage checks, model comparison, explanation and monitoring. Compare it with controlled non-agent analysis and human review. The paper was labelled "under review" on its arXiv page; no peer-reviewed status was verified during this task.
