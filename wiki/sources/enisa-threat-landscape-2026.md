## Summary

The [ENISA Threat Landscape 2026](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/enisa-threat-landscape-2026) analyses incidents and events observed in the EU from 1 January to 31 December 2025. It is contextual evidence for selecting security problems, not a downloadable labelled ML corpus.

## Key points

- The publication page dates the report 22 September 2026 and links the full PDF.
- ENISA points to geopolitical factors and digital interconnection as context for its EU threat analysis; the landing page alone does not substantiate numeric rankings for specific threat types.
- The earlier [2025 edition](https://www.enisa.europa.eu/publications/enisa-threat-landscape-2025) covers a different observation window (July 2024-June 2025) and has a corrected v1.3 published 22 September 2026. Do not mix editions when claiming trends.

## Details

### Evidence limits

This record uses the official publication summaries, not a complete review of their PDFs or incident-collection methodology. Reported incidents are not a random sample of all attacks, nor labelled flow or endpoint training examples. Check the report's methods and category definitions before using proportions or comparing years.
