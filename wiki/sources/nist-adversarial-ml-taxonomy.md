## Summary

[NIST AI 100-2 E2025](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/ai/100/2/e2025/final) is a terminology and taxonomy report for adversarial machine learning, organised around ML methods, attack lifecycle stages, attacker goals and mitigations.

## Key points

- NIST identifies poisoning, evasion, abuse and privacy breaches among the vocabulary needed for threat modelling; these are categories, not measured rates of attacks.
- The publication page records a corrected PDF and a potential-update notice. Review errata before using precise definitions from the full text.

## Details

### Research use and limits

Use the taxonomy to separate attacker access and objective when studying a defender model or agent. This page is based on the publication landing page and abstract, not an independent analysis of every mitigation in the PDF. A taxonomy is not an efficacy benchmark for any defence.
