## Summary

[MITRE ATT&CK](https://attack.mitre.org/) is a public knowledge base of adversary tactics and techniques based on observations. It provides a common vocabulary for threat modelling and detection coverage, not a labelled benchmark of model accuracy.

## Key points

- ATT&CK can help classify defensive analytic tasks by adversary behaviour and available telemetry.
- A technique mapping is not proof that a detector works or that a threat occurs at a known frequency.

## Details

### Research use and limits

When mapping a classifier to ATT&CK, specify the observable behaviour, telemetry source, and tested environment. The website is updated over time; pin the consulted release before comparing mappings longitudinally. This page does not claim to have checked ATT&CK dataset downloads or all technique definitions.
