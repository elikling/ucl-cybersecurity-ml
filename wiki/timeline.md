## Summary

Selected dated anchors for the current evidence set. Collection date, paper submission, publication date and repository status are different kinds of events.

## Key points

- **2015:** UNSW-NB15 publication referenced by the [UNSW dataset page](sources/unsw-nb15-documentation.md); page itself updated in 2021.
- **3-7 July 2017:** [CIC-IDS2017](sources/cic-ids2017-documentation.md) network capture period.
- **2017-18:** feature-release periods for [EMBER](sources/ember-project.md), not a statement about present-day malware.
- **2020:** [SoReL-20M](sources/sorel-20m-project.md) project/paper release.
- **19 April 2024:** [CyberSecEval 2](sources/cyberseceval-2-preprint.md) preprint submission.
- **24 March 2025:** [NIST AI 100-2 E2025](sources/nist-adversarial-ml-taxonomy.md) final report; corrected PDF and potential-update notice followed in April and June 2025.
- **5 October 2025:** [LLM-based data-science agents survey](sources/data-science-agents-survey.md) preprint submission, marked under review.
- **13 April 2026:** [EMBER repository](sources/ember-project.md) archived.
- **22 September 2026:** [ENISA Threat Landscape 2026](sources/enisa-threat-landscape-2026.md) published, describing EU incidents observed in calendar year 2025; the older 2025 report received a correction the same day.
- **7 October 2026:** this wiki's source checks and first synthesis; not a publication date for undated living software documentation.

## Details

The [Verizon 2026 DBIR](sources/verizon-dbir-2026.md) landing page does not give a publication day. The [TabPFN](sources/tabpfn-project.md), [CybersecurityBenchmarks](sources/cyberseceval-repository.md), [PyOD](sources/pyod-project.md), [Sigma](sources/sigma-project.md), [Zeek](sources/zeek-project.md) and [Suricata](sources/suricata-project.md) repository pages are living references. Pin releases and commit hashes before using them as time-series evidence; no release timeline is inferred from an access date.
